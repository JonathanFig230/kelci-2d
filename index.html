How to Play

Goal

Kelci starts out tiny. Walk into things smaller than her to eat them and grow. Keep growing until Kelci is big enough to eat the entire world.

Controls

Move with WASD or the Arrow Keys (or the on-screen pad on touch devices). Just walk into anything Kelci can eat — no extra button needed.

Growing

Every object has a size. Kelci can only eat things her size or smaller. Eating fills her growth bar; once it's full, she levels up, gets bigger, and unlocks bigger things to eat. Things too big to eat will block her path until she's grown enough.

Explore

The world isn't just scattered at random — a forest, a suburb, a city, a military base, a farm, a desert, a lakeside, and an airfield each have their own things to eat, with mountains guarding the approach to the World itself.

Finding the World

Somewhere out there sits the entire world, waiting. Use the minimap in the corner to find it — it's marked with a gold star. You'll need to grow enormous before you can take a bite.

Moodeng

Kelci's pygmy-hippo companion follows her everywhere and grows right along with her. Press M (or the button in the corner) to hop on — riding Moodeng makes Kelci noticeably faster.
